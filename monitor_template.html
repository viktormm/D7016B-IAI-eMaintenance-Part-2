Track and overhead line against limits

One LiDAR scan, one curve of the track. The gauge of the rails and the height and stagger of the contact wire are read along the chainage, compared with reference limits, and watched with Western Electric control-chart rules. A last tab lets the metres sort themselves into states.

Settings

Every metre of track gets one feature vector from what the pipeline measured. The metres are grouped into states by k-means (unsupervised: no labels, no assumptions about what a state is), and each metre gets an anomaly score, its distance from the centre of its own state. The states and the score are recomputed here as you change the settings.
