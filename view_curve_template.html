Notes

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The camera icon above the plot saves a PNG. Click a legend entry to hide it.

Track frame: chainage s along the reference line, sideways offset d from it (left = +), and height Z as stored in the LiDAR file. The rails are 1.43 m apart in a track hundreds of metres long, so sideways and height are stretched; ×1 is true scale. Narrowing the window or hiding the point cloud keeps the shape of the box.

World: E and N as in the file with the origin subtracted (see the axis titles), true horizontal scale; only height is stretched.

Lines are the Catmull-Rom curve through the control points (circles); the thick line midway between the rails is the centreline (red for one run, in each run's own colour when several are shown). The purple bar is the cursor. Z is relative to the sensor, so the LiDAR track's own rails stay nearly flat.

Runs over time (three or more runs): the first run stays on screen as the baseline and one later run is shown against it; the slider or Play steps through the runs. The changes are millimetres, so the later run's sideways change against the baseline is drawn larger than it is (slider; ×1 is true size) – its position on screen is then not to scale. The cursor table always gives the measured values, and its last row is the change between the two runs in mm. Colour by change colours the later run's rails by their gauge change and its centreline by its sideways change (orange: wider or to the left, blue: narrower or to the right); the baseline is grey. If the runs carry findings (simulated monitoring), the alerts and alarms of the run on screen are listed under the slider and marked as a strip under the track (amber: alert, red: alarm); click one to go to that stretch.
